Welcome to IIofA

A Home for Faith, Family & Community

The Islamic Institute of America, founded in 2015, is located in Dearborn Heights, Michigan. Established by Imam Sayed Hassan Qazwini and funded by community donations, the institute is made up of over 1,000 families with diverse ethnicities.

  • Daily prayers & Jumma services
  • Quran & Islamic education for all ages
  • Youth programs and community events
  • Interfaith outreach and dialogue
